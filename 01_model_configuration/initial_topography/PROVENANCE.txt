Provenance of the grids in this folder
=====================================

These five CSV grids are NOT the original model input files. The originals
(data/fan.csv, data/fan100.csv, ...) were not archived with the model output and
could not be recovered.

The grids here were rebuilt with 01_model_configuration/export_initial_dem.py,
which reads the base of the first stratigraphic layer from the model's own
output (h5/sed.time1.hdf5, layElev[:, 0]). They therefore reproduce the initial
topographic surface that the five runs actually started from, sampled on the
500 m regular grid of the stratigraphic output (499 x 399 nodes, X = 0-249 km,
Y = 0-199 km, elevation -2560 m to +2297 m).

The five grids differ only in the height of the elliptical dome that represents
the salt diapir (0, 100, 200, 250 and 300 m; 20 km x 40 km, centred at
X = 160 km, Y = 100 km). Every other element - the shelf and slope in the west,
the 8 km wide canyon cut at X = 130 km, Y = 100 km, and the eastern basin - is
identical.

Note for a re-run: the sea floor in the reported simulations was resolved by the
model TIN (about 40 000 nodes, mean spacing just over 1 km). A run started from
these 500 m grids uses a finer surface and will not reproduce the published
numbers exactly.

If the original data/fan*.csv files are recovered, replace this folder with them
and delete this note.
